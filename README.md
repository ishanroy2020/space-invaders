# Pixel Siege

A neon retro arcade shooter that runs in any phone or desktop browser.

**Play:** https://ishanroy2020.github.io/space-invaders/

## How to play

- **Two thumbs (default on phones):** drag on the left half to move, hold on the right half to fire.
- **One thumb:** switch with the controls button in the menu or pause screen; drag anywhere to move, auto-fire while touching.
- **Keyboard:** arrows or A/D to move, Space to fire, B for smart bomb, P to pause.
- **Smart bomb:** fills up as you destroy enemies and graze bullets; tap the BOMB button when it glows.
